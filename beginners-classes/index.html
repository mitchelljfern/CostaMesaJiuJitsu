Frequently asked questions

Good to know

Do I need any experience to join?
None. That's the whole point. Everyone in this program is starting on day one with you, so nobody is ahead of you and nobody is watching you struggle. If you've never touched a mat before, you're exactly who this was built for.
Is $99 the total price?
Yes, $99 covers the full 6 weeks. No signup fee, no contract, no auto-renew.
When does the program start?
Any week. It's an ongoing program, so you can join on any Tuesday. Your 6 weeks start at your first class.
Will I be sparring or rolling live right away?
No. Your first classes are movement, position, and safety. Live rolling can be introduced later and is totally optional, only when you're ready for it. You will never get thrown into something you haven't been taught.
What happens when the 6 weeks are done?
You'll have a real foundation and a decision to make. Most people roll into a regular membership and keep training with the same crew they started with. There's no obligation and no pressure.
What if I miss a class?
Life happens. We'll get you caught up at the next class. Your 6 weeks keep running from your first class, so missed classes can't be saved or added on at the end.
I'm out of shape. Should I get in better shape first?
No. Waiting until you're in shape to start is how people never start. The program is built for beginners and you go at your own pace. Jiu jitsu is what gets you in shape, not the other way around.
Who's teaching the program?
Anthony Misitano, a black belt with more than a decade of experience on the mats. He's coached hundreds of people through their first classes. This isn't a class handed off to an assistant.
What does a class actually look like?
Warm up, technique of the day, drilling with a partner, then positional work. Every class builds on the last one so nothing comes out of nowhere. Classes are 60 minutes.
