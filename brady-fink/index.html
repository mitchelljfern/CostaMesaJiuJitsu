Coach Brady Fink

Brady Fink is a Brazilian Jiu Jitsu Black Belt & Martial Artist with over two decades of experience in competing in Brazilian Jiu Jitsu, MMA, and coaching at the highest levels of the sport.

About Brady

Brady combines his technical expertise with years of experience training elite fighters, making him a sought-after mentor in the martial arts world.

Brady has played a pivotal role in the careers of some of the top fighters globally, including former UFC Middleweight Champion Michael Bisping. His deep understanding of MMA striking, grappling, and strategy has helped fighters excel in the Octagon and beyond.

With a proven history of guiding both champions and everyday practitioners, Brady's coaching provides an inspiring and supportive environment for anyone looking to grow in martial arts.

At a glance

  • Brazilian Jiu Jitsu black belt
  • 20+ years competing in BJJ and MMA
  • Coached former UFC Middleweight Champion Michael Bisping
