Why an octopus

An octopus is the grappler of the sea. Eight limbs, all grip, no wasted energy, and it wins by staying calm and controlling position. That is jiu jitsu. The black belt in its tentacles says the rest: patience, skill and years on the mat.

09

Do and don't

Most logo mistakes are one of these. When in doubt, use the file as it comes.

Do

Use the full-color logo on black, deep teal or white, with room around it.

Do

On photos, darken the image and use the white one-color logo.

Do

Switch to the octopus head when space is tight.

Don't stretch

Always scale from a corner so it keeps its shape.

Don't recolor

The octopus is cyan with red eyes. No team colors, no holiday colors.

Don't drop it on busy photos

Darken the photo or use a solid background.

Don't add effects

No extra outlines, shadows, glows or bevels. It already has its rim.

Don't crop the logo

Need just the octopus? Use the octopus head file instead.

Don't fade it

Full strength or not at all. No see-through watermarks.
