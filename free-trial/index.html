Questions

Good to know

Is the free class really free?
Yes. One class, any program, no card required and no pressure to join. The only thing it does not cover is the beginners jiu jitsu program, which is its own $99 program.
Do I need any experience?
No. Most of our students started with zero experience, and the coaches walk you through everything. If you want a slower on-ramp, the beginners jiu jitsu program is built exactly for that.
What should I wear?
Athletic clothes with no zippers, buttons or pockets. A rash guard and shorts work great. Bring a water bottle.
How old do kids need to be?
Little Ninjas starts at age 4. Youth Jiu Jitsu is ages 7 to 12. Teens 13 and up train in the adult classes.
Where are you?
Inside Double Ops at 369 E 17th St, Suite 10, Costa Mesa, in the Triangle Square area, with parking right outside.
