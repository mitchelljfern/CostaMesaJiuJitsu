Good to know

Do I need any experience to start?
No. Most of our students started with zero experience. Your first class is free and the coaches walk you through everything. If you want a slower on-ramp, the beginners jiu jitsu program is built exactly for that.
What should I wear to my first class?
Athletic clothes with no zippers, buttons or pockets. For NoGi, a rash guard and shorts work great. Gi classes need a kimono, which we sell in adult and kid sizes, but athletic clothes are fine for your free class. Bring a water bottle.
How old do kids need to be?
Little Ninjas starts at age 4. Youth Jiu Jitsu is ages 7 to 12. Teens 13 and up train in the adult classes.
Is the free class really free?
Yes. One class, any program, no card required and no pressure to join. The only thing it does not cover is the beginners jiu jitsu program, which is its own $99 program.
Is jiu jitsu safe?
Jiu jitsu is a grappling art, so there is no striking in our jiu jitsu classes. We teach tapping, posture and injury prevention from day one, and beginners never roll live until they are ready.
Can I just drop in?
Yes, and we encourage it. Show up about 10 minutes before class. If it is your first time, booking the free class above helps us have a coach ready for you.
Are there contracts?
No long-term contracts. Memberships are monthly, the 10-class pack is a one-time purchase, and the beginners program is a one-time $99 for 6 weeks.
Do you offer private lessons?
Yes, for kids and adults with Coach Tony. Send a quick request or call or text 949-556-1178, and he will set it up around your schedule.
Where are you and where do I park?
We are inside Double Ops at 369 E 17th St, Suite 10, Costa Mesa, in the Triangle Square area, with parking right outside.
