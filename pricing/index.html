Questions

Good to know

Are there contracts?
No long-term contracts. Memberships are monthly, the 10-class pack is a one-time purchase, and the beginners program is a one-time $99 for 6 weeks.
Is there a sign-up fee?
No. You pay for the membership or pack you choose, and that's it.
Can I try a class before I buy?
Yes. Your first class is free, any program, kids or adults. Book your free class.
How does the Family Add-On work?
Add a second family member to any unlimited membership for $120 a month. Call or text 949-556-1178 or email info@costamesajiujitsu.com to set it up.
How much are private lessons?
Send a quick request and Coach Tony will walk you through pricing and options before anything is booked.
