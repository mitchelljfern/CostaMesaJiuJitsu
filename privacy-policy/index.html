Privacy Policy

Costa Mesa Jiu Jitsu ("CMJJ," "we," "us") is operated by Costa Mesa Dojo LLC. This policy explains how we collect, use, and share information when you visit our website, use our member app, train with us, or join our text messaging program.

Text messaging program

The program name is Costa Mesa Jiu Jitsu. If you opt in, we may text you class reminders, schedule changes, membership/billing notices, replies to inquiries, and occasional promotions. Message frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out and HELP for help.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties/affiliates for marketing/promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties.

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. Consent to receive texts is not a condition of purchase or of joining a class.

For help with texts, email info@costamesajiujitsu.com or call or text (949) 556-1178. Our SMS Terms are at SMS Terms.

What we collect

  • Information you give us: name, email, phone number, date of birth or age group, emergency contact, and, for parents, the children on your account.
  • Membership details: plan, status, belt rank, attendance, class bookings, waitlists, and waivers or membership agreements you accept.
  • Messages you send us, including texts and in-app messages, and our replies.
  • Payments, which are handled by Stripe on Stripe's own pages. We see your plan, payment status, and the last four digits and expiry date of your card. We never see or store your full card number.
  • App sign-in and security data, such as a one-time sign-in PIN (stored only in scrambled form and expiring after a short time), a sign-in cookie, and IP addresses used to limit repeated sign-in attempts.
  • Push notification tokens, if you allow notifications in the member app, plus device type and app version.
  • Website and app use, such as pages visited and basic device information, to keep the site working and secure.

We do not use advertising trackers to sell your information, and we do not sell or rent your information.

How we use it

  • To run your membership: sign-in, class booking, waitlists, billing, freezes, and cancellations.
  • To communicate with you about classes, scheduling, membership, billing, and questions you ask us, including by text if you have opted in.
  • To keep the gym, website, and app secure, and to meet legal, tax, and safety requirements.

Children

Children do not sign in to the member app. A parent or guardian signs in and books classes for their children. We use a child's name, age, program, belt rank, and attendance only to run their classes. Kids' text messages go to the linked parent or guardian, not to the child. Parents can ask to review, correct, or delete their child's information at info@costamesajiujitsu.com.

Who helps us

We share information only with service providers that run parts of our website, app, billing, or messaging for us, and only for that purpose. That includes hosting, member records, payment processing, email, and text delivery (including Twilio once our own texting number is in use). We may also share information if the law requires it, or to protect the safety of our members and staff.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties/affiliates for marketing/promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties.

How long we keep it

We keep member information while you are a member and afterwards as long as we need it for billing, tax, legal, and safety reasons. Sign-in PINs expire after a short time.

Deleting your app account

If you use the member app, you can delete your app account: open Membership, then tap Delete my app account. When you do, you are signed out, and that email cannot sign in again unless you ask us to restore it. Your in-app messages and push notification tokens are deleted. We may still need contact details if you have an active membership or a balance. We keep payment, membership agreement, and attendance records that we have to keep for billing, tax, or legal reasons. Deleting your app account doesn't cancel your gym membership or stop billing. To cancel your membership, use Cancel membership in the app or contact the front desk.

Your choices

  • Reply STOP to opt out of texts, or HELP for help.
  • Turn push notifications off in your phone's settings.
  • Ask us for a copy of your information, or to correct or delete it, by emailing info@costamesajiujitsu.com. We may need to confirm it is you first.

Cookies and tracking technologies

We use cookies and similar tracking technologies to improve your browsing experience and analyze website traffic. You can modify your browser settings to disable cookies, but some features of our website may not function properly.

Your privacy rights

If you are a California resident, you may have rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), including:

  • The right to know what personal data we collect.

  • The right to request deletion of your personal information.

  • The right to opt out of the sale of personal information (though we do not sell your data).

To exercise these rights, please contact us using the information below.

Data security

We implement reasonable security measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access or disclosure. However, no online system is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Third-party links

Our website may contain links to third-party websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites and encourage you to review their privacy policies.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and post the new version here.
