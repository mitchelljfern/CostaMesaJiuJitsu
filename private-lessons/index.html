Good to know

How much are private lessons?
Pricing varies by coach. Send a request and the coach will walk you through options before anything is booked.
Who are private lessons for?
Kids and adults at any level, from nervous first-timers who want a head start to competitors fixing a specific position.
Can I bring a friend or family member?
Yes. Lessons can be solo, with a friend, or as a family.
Where are lessons held?
On the mats inside Double Ops at 369 E 17th St, Suite 10, Costa Mesa.
