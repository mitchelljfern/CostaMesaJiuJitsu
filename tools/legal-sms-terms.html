SMS Terms

These terms govern the text messaging program operated by Costa Mesa Jiu Jitsu ("CMJJ," "we," "us"), which is operated by Costa Mesa Dojo LLC. The program name is Costa Mesa Jiu Jitsu. By providing your mobile number and opting in, you agree to these terms.

What we text

If you opt in, we may send you class reminders, schedule changes, membership/billing notices, replies to inquiries, and occasional promotions. Message frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out and HELP for help.

Consent to receive marketing text messages is not a condition of purchasing any goods or services, or of joining a class.

How you opt in

You can opt in by checking a box on a Costa Mesa Jiu Jitsu website or lead form when you give us your mobile number, or by giving your number in person at the gym and agreeing to receive texts. Kids' messages go to the linked parent or guardian, not to the child.

How you opt out or get help

Reply STOP to opt out. After you text STOP, we will send one confirmation and then stop texting that number. You can also text STOPALL, UNSUBSCRIBE, CANCEL, END, or QUIT. To opt in again later, reply START or sign up again through an approved method.

Reply HELP for help, or email info@costamesajiujitsu.com or call or text (949) 556-1178.

Carriers and delivery

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. Costa Mesa Jiu Jitsu does not charge a separate fee for the texting program, but your wireless carrier's standard messaging and data charges may apply.

Privacy

No mobile information will be shared with third parties/affiliates for marketing/promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties.
