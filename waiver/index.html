Liability waiver

Every student needs a signed waiver on file before training. It takes about two minutes. Parents or guardians sign for anyone under 18.

Release of Liability, Assumption of Risk and Agreement Not to Sue

Please read this carefully. By signing, you give up legal rights, including the right to sue.

1. Activities

This waiver covers all classes, open mats, private lessons, events, competitions and any other use of the facility or equipment at Costa Mesa Jiu Jitsu, including Brazilian jiu jitsu (Gi and NoGi), grappling, striking, kickboxing, boxing, conditioning, strength training and related activities ("the Activities").

2. I understand the risks

Martial arts and fitness training are physical contact activities. I understand the Activities carry risks of injury, including but not limited to sprains, strains, cuts, bruises, dislocations, broken bones, concussions, joint and spinal injuries, infections, heart attack, paralysis and death. These risks can come from my own actions, the actions of training partners, coaches or others, the condition of the facility or equipment, or from the normal nature of the training itself. I voluntarily choose to take part and I accept all of these risks, known and unknown.

3. Release and waiver of claims

To the fullest extent allowed by law, I release, waive and discharge Costa Mesa Jiu Jitsu, Double Ops, and their owners, coaches, instructors, employees, volunteers and landlord (together, "the Gym") from any and all claims, demands, losses and liability for any injury, illness, death, or damage to property that happens to me while taking part in the Activities or while on the premises, including claims caused by the ordinary negligence of the Gym.

4. Agreement not to sue

I agree not to sue the Gym, or bring any claim against the Gym, for any injury, death or loss covered by this release. If I or anyone on my behalf brings such a claim, I agree to pay the Gym's reasonable legal fees and costs.

5. Indemnity

I agree to defend and hold the Gym harmless from any claims brought by me, my family, my heirs or anyone else that arise from my participation in the Activities.

6. My health

I confirm that I am physically able to take part, that I have no medical condition that would make participation unsafe for me or others, and that I will tell a coach about any injury or condition before training. I will stop and tell a coach if I feel unwell or get hurt.

7. Medical treatment

If I am injured or unable to respond, I authorize the Gym to call for and get emergency medical care for me. I am responsible for any medical costs.

8. Rules

I agree to follow the coaches' instructions and the Gym's rules, including tapping when caught in a submission, respecting training partners' taps, keeping nails short and gear clean, and not training with skin infections.

9. Minors

If the participant is under 18, I am their parent or legal guardian. I agree to everything in this waiver on the child's behalf and on my own behalf, and I agree to the release, agreement not to sue and indemnity for any claim brought by or for the child.

10. General

This waiver stays in effect for all of my future participation unless I revoke it in writing. It is governed by California law. If any part is found unenforceable, the rest stays in full effect. I have read this waiver, I understand it, and I sign it voluntarily.
